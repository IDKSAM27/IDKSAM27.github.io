## Overview

In *Mythos*, Stephen Fry brings the ancient Greek myths into the modern era with his trademark wit, intelligence, and deep affection for the lore. Starting from the primordial void of Chaos and the emergence of Gaia and Uranus, Fry chronicles the rise of the Titans, the bloody overthrow by Zeus and the Olympians, and the creation of humankind.

---

## Core Themes & Mythic Elements

### 1. From Primordial Chaos to Divine Order
The Greek cosmos begins not with a benevolent creation, but with raw elements—Chaos, Gaia (Earth), Erebus, and Nyx (Night). The transition from Titan rule to Olympian dominance is a cosmic drama of ambition, jealousy, and fate.

### 2. The Flawed Humanity of Gods
Unlike all-powerful deities of monotheistic traditions, the Greek gods are petty, passionate, vengeful, and hilarious. Zeus's impulsive desires, Hera's sharp vengeance, and Prometheus's heroic defiance reflect human virtues and vices writ large across the heavens.

### 3. The Gift of Fire and Hope
Prometheus's stealing of fire from Olympus to bestow upon humanity remains one of the most enduring metaphors for civilization, technology, and intellectual awakening.
