## Overview

Set in the Roaring Twenties on Long Island, F. Scott Fitzgerald's *The Great Gatsby* explores the decadence, idealism, social upheaval, and excess of the Jazz Age through the eyes of narrator Nick Carraway as he becomes entangled with his enigmatic neighbor, Jay Gatsby, and Gatsby's obsessive pursuit of Daisy Buchanan.
